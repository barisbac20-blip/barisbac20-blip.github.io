The Profile

Precision as a habit.

I'm Barış — a student at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, studying Electrical Engineering and Information Technology. I began in Mechanical Engineering and switched tracks after two semesters, deliberately, because electrical systems, information technology, and programming were where my real interest lived. That decision defines how I work: depth in the things that matter, and no claims I can't back.

My instruments are modern C++ (C++17/20) and Python. KIT project work taught me to treat testing and build tooling as part of the craft: CMake, Catch2, Git-based teamwork, and CI pipelines are part of my normal workflow, not an afterthought. My favorite piece so far is Wordlomat, a Wordle engine whose entropy-based solver averages 3.4 guesses; it scored 100% on KIT's automated grader. On the Python side: an AI-assisted anomaly-detection pipeline (FastAPI, pytest, Docker) that I set up, tested, and verified end to end — plus a personal automation stack that runs my study and admin life on macOS.

Beyond coursework I do volunteer IT support for Engineers Without Borders Karlsruhe — where I rolled out a digital-signature workflow for a distributed volunteer team — and mentor international students through KIT's Buddy Programme. The trajectory points toward data, AI, and quantitative research. I work in German, English, and Turkish.

Every project claim on this page is backed by tests, repositories, or certificates.
